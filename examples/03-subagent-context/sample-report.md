Internal Grid Operations Briefing (fictional sample data)

Battery storage deployment reached 4.2 GWh in the service region this year, a 30% increase
over the prior year. Deployment growth is concentrated in utility-scale sites rather than
residential systems.

--- Page 2 ---

Curtailment events — periods where generated renewable power could not be used or stored —
rose 15% year-over-year. The operations team attributes this to storage build-out lagging
behind new solar and wind capacity additions by roughly one construction cycle.

--- Page 3 ---

Second-life battery pilots (repurposing retired EV battery packs for stationary storage)
are running at two sites. Early results show retained capacity of 70-78% after automotive
service life, in line with third-party estimates.
